For independent lodges and camps

Your sightings are your best sales tool.

Travellers tell us which species they most want to see. We send them to the camps where guides actually see those species, in the months they can travel. That favours the camps with a genuine wildlife story, not the biggest marketing budgets.

How partnership works

First

Share what you already keep

A photo or export of your guides' sighting log each month, with the number of drives. No new systems and no double entry for your guides.

Then

We send matched guests

Travellers whose wishlist matches your species are routed to you, through your agent terms or our safari specialist partner, each with a Wishlist Safari reference.

Finally

Guests arrive wanting what you have

Guests come for what your camp does best, so expectations are realistic and reviews are stronger.

The commercial terms

  • Cost to listFree.
  • Our feeA referral commission, agreed with each partner, paid only on guests who travel. Nothing on enquiries, nothing up front.
  • How bookings flowDirectly with you under your standard agent terms, or through our specialist operator partner, who contracts with you as normal.
  • TrackingEvery guest carries a Wishlist Safari reference, recorded on the booking.
  • ExclusivityNone. Keep every channel you already use.
  • Your dataStays yours. We use it only to calculate species patterns by camp and month. Never live locations, and never the whereabouts of rhino, pangolin or other sensitive species.

Founding partners

We are signing a small group of independent camps across southern and central Africa for launch, chosen for the species they are known for. Founding partners are featured first in our species guides.

We are especially keen to hear from camps known for

Brown hyena, pangolin, aardvark, wild dog, Pel's fishing owl, shoebill, sitatunga, Thornicroft's giraffe, roan and sable, and the great primate and bird specialities of Zambia, Malawi, Rwanda and Uganda.
