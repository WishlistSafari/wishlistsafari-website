Wishlist Sightings · For safari guides

Your sightings deserve to be seen.

You see more in a week than most travellers see in a lifetime. Send us your sightings and photos from the drive. Your camp gets a ready content library, travellers see what your camp really offers, and you are credited and rewarded for what you share.

How it works

First

Share a sighting

After a drive, add the species, the day and a photo or video link below. It takes under a minute, and your details are remembered on your phone for next time.

Then

Your camp gets the content

We tag and sort everything into a weekly gallery for your camp to use on its social channels and with its agents. Nothing is published without your camp's approval.

Finally

You are credited and rewarded

Your name goes on every photo we use, and you share in the Guide Fund and in any income from licensing your clips.

How guides are rewarded

  • The Guide Fund10% of every referral fee Wishlist Safari earns from your camp goes to the guides who shared sightings from that camp, split by the sightings accepted that month.
  • Clip licensingWhen a media outlet or channel pays to use one of your photos or videos, you receive 50% of what we are paid.
  • CreditYour name on every image we publish, on our site and on your camp's channels.
  • PaymentPaid monthly once you have earnings, by bank transfer, mobile money, PayPal or Wise. We confirm your payment details privately by email or WhatsApp, never on this form.

The rules of the road

Good guiding comes first. We only accept sightings that follow your reserve's rules and ethical guiding practice.

  • Never send exact locations. We only ever show the camp and month.
  • We never publish anything about where rhino, pangolin or other sensitive species are.
  • No baiting, calling in, chasing or off-road driving where it is not allowed.
  • Only send photos and videos you took yourself.
  • Guests' faces only with their permission.
