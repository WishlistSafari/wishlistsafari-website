How it works

Your wishlist

From the Big Five to a shoebill or a Pel's fishing owl. Choose as many as you like.

Honest matching

We match your wishlist to the places each species is reliably found, built from guides' own sighting records as our partner camps join.

Book with the camp

We introduce you straight to each camp's own reservations team. No middleman, and you deal with the people who know the place best.

Wildlife first

We never share live locations, and never publish where rhino or pangolin were seen.
