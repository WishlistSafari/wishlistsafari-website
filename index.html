About Wishlist Safari

Built for travellers who dream in species

Most safari sites sell places. But people dream in animals: the leopard on a branch, the shoebill in the reeds, the pangolin they have waited a lifetime to see. Wishlist Safari starts there, and points you to where each one is most reliably seen.

We are independent and based in Cape Town, and we especially look for smaller, family-run and community-owned camps whose guides know their wildlife best.

What we are, and are not

  • We match your wishlist and introduce you directly to the camps.
  • We are not a tour operator or travel agent. We never design itineraries, sell trips or take payments.
  • You book with the camp, at its own direct rate. A camp pays us a referral fee only if you travel, and it never adds to your price.
  • Questions? Write to hello@wishlistsafari.com.
